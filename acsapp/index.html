Feature 01

Home picks up where you left off.

Open the app and your next step is already waiting: the class you didn't finish, today's study tasks, and the live classes coming up.

  • Continue watching
  • Today's plan
  • Upcoming live classes
  • Top courses
Feature 02

Courses and lessons, neatly organised.

Every course follows the same path, from Course to Subject to Chapter to Class, so students always know where they are.

  • Course
  • Subject
  • Chapter
  • Class
Feature 03

A lesson player built for revision.

Play lessons at 0.5× to 3× speed, choose the quality, and continue every lesson where you stopped. PDF notes, comments and reactions sit under each lesson, and screen recording is blocked while it plays.

  • 0.5× to 3× speed
  • Choice of quality
  • Resume anywhere
  • PDF notes
  • Comments and reactions
  • Protected content
Feature 04

Class Snap: capture a moment, then ask about it.

One tap saves the frame of the lesson you are watching. Save it to the phone, post it in Community, or ask the AI about it, with the subject and chapter filled in for you.

  • Snap
  • Share
  • Ask AI
Feature 05

Live Class that feels like a real classroom.

Chat with a math keyboard for equations, answer the teacher's live quiz, raise your hand to speak, or hold push-to-talk. Choose the Classic engine or the Modern one, built natively for Android, with low delay that stays steady on weak networks.

  • Live chat
  • Live Quiz
  • Raise hand
  • Push-to-talk
  • Class materials
  • Realtime video
Feature 06

Lab: 53 simulations to explore by hand.

Each simulation follows an HSC (NCTB) chapter, is explained in English and Bangla, and works offline: projectile motion, titration, mitosis, conic sections and more. ICT adds HTML, SQL and C playgrounds to write code and run it on the phone.

  • Physics 19
  • Chemistry 11
  • Biology 11
  • Mathematics 12
  • ICT coding lab
Feature 07

ACS Calculator: a full scientific calculator.

Modelled on the fx-991CW, key for key. It gives exact answers like the textbook (√8 = 2√2, 0.1 + 0.2 = 3/10), turns any calculation into a QR code, and works fully offline.

  • Statistics
  • Equation
  • Matrix
  • Vector
  • Complex
  • Base-N
  • Distribution
  • Table
  • Inequality
  • Ratio
Feature 08

Sit exams right in the app.

Exams open from Profile in one tap, sign you in automatically, and are built for the phone screen.

  • One tap from Profile
  • Automatic sign-in
  • Built for the phone
Feature 09

Plan every day with the Study Planner.

See what to study today, the days ahead, upcoming exams and leave days. Today's plan also appears right on Home.

  • Today
  • Schedule
  • Exams
  • Leave days
Feature 10

Community: study together.

Feed, Discussion and For you bring together posts from your groups, people you follow and ACS. Each batch and class has its own group with a notice board, plus Stories, Reels, polls and anonymous questions checked by a moderator. The notes library, homework and teachers' materials live here too.

  • Groups
  • Stories and Reels
  • Polls
  • Anonymous questions
  • Notes library
  • Homework
  • Materials
Feature 11

Download once, watch anywhere.

Every downloaded class is protected with AES-256 encryption and plays only inside the app, on your own account. Pick a quality from 240p to 1080p and see the space it needs; downloads continue in the background, with progress in the notification.

  • AES-256 encryption
  • 240p to 1080p
  • Background downloads
  • Your account only
Feature 12

Focus Mode: study without distractions.

Choose how much of Community to hold back, for a set time or until you turn it off.

  • Basic: hides Reels and Stories
  • Pro: only teacher and moderator posts
  • Ultra: hides Community and its notifications
